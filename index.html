About Nchito Community Initiative

Transforming lives from the ghetto to greatness

🚀 Our Mission

To uplift and empower young people from marginalized communities in Zambia by providing them with job training, life skills, mentorship, and access to employment opportunities. We are committed to instilling hope, fostering peace, and equipping youth with the tools they need to lead productive and dignified lives.

🌟 Our Vision

A Zambia where every young person, regardless of background, has equal access to growth, employment, peace, and a hopeful future.

📅 Our History

Nchito Community Initiative was founded in Lusaka with the desire to break the cycles of poverty and exclusion affecting youth in disadvantaged neighborhoods. Recognizing that many young people are overlooked by society, we created a platform to teach them how to fish, not just give them the fish.

Our Programs

Comprehensive initiatives designed to empower Zambian youth

Skills for Life Training

Hands-on training in trades and digital skills, targeted at unemployed and unskilled youth.

  • Vocational training in tailoring, carpentry, ICT, hairdressing
  • Digital skills and computer literacy
  • Entrepreneurship and business management

Youth Employment & Job Linkages

Job matching, CV development, and employer partnerships to transition youth into the workforce.

  • CV creation and interview preparation
  • Connections with local companies
  • Internship and apprenticeship programs

Youth Mentorship & Motivation

Guidance from role models and community leaders who inspire and build confidence.

  • Regular mentorship sessions
  • Motivational speaking events
  • Personal development workshops

Diaspora Engagement

Mobilizing Zambians abroad to invest in youth-led initiatives through donations, mentorship, and skill exchange.

  • Diaspora mentorship programs
  • Skill exchange initiatives
  • Investment opportunities

Community Impact Projects

Environmental cleanups, youth-led peace campaigns, and awareness programs in local communities.

  • Community cleanups
  • Peace and civic education
  • Youth-led awareness campaigns
